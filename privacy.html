Privacy Policy

Bold Men of God
Effective Date: October 24, 2026

Bold Men of God ("we," "our," or "the app") is a daily devotional app for men, published by Fedhound LLC. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

The short version: everything you enter in the app stays on your phone. The only information that leaves your device is what is needed to process and verify your purchase, described below.

· · ✦ · ·

Information Stored on Your Device

The app keeps the following information only on your device:

  • Your first name, entered during setup and used to personalize the app.
  • Your reading progress, so the app knows which devotional comes next.
  • Your bookmarks, the devotionals you choose to save.
  • Your settings, such as your preferred Bible translation.

This information is never sent to us or to any server. We cannot see it. If you uninstall the app, it is deleted from your device.

All devotionals and Bible verses are built into the app, so it works fully offline. The app does not require an account or login.

· · ✦ · ·

Purchases

The full library is unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase. There is no subscription.

  • Google Play processes your payment under Google's own privacy policy. We never receive or store your payment card information.
  • RevenueCat manages the purchase on our behalf so the app can verify it and restore it if you reinstall. RevenueCat receives your purchase record (the product purchased, transaction details, and purchase date) and an anonymous app user ID that is randomly generated and not linked to your name or email. It may also receive limited technical information needed to handle these requests, such as the app version, device platform, and IP address. (RevenueCat Privacy Policy)

An internet connection is used only when you make or restore a purchase.

· · ✦ · ·

What We Do Not Collect

The app contains no ads, no advertising identifiers, no analytics or tracking tools, and no location access. We do not ask for your email address, contacts, or any other personal information.

· · ✦ · ·

Sharing Verses

When you share a verse, the app hands the verse and any note you add to the app you choose (for example, your messaging or email app) through your phone's standard share menu. We do not receive or store anything you share.

· · ✦ · ·

We Never Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade information about you. The purchase information described above is used only to process, verify, and restore your purchase.

· · ✦ · ·

Children's Privacy

Bold Men of God is intended for adult men and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has made a purchase or provided information through the app, contact us and we will help.

· · ✦ · ·

Your Choices

  • Information on your device: uninstalling the app deletes your name, reading progress, bookmarks, and settings.
  • Purchase records: you can ask us to delete the purchase record held by RevenueCat by emailing us. Records held by Google Play are governed by Google's policies. Deleting the RevenueCat record may prevent automatic restore of your purchase.
  • Questions: email us anytime at the address below.
· · ✦ · ·

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will change the Effective Date at the top of this page. If a change affects what information leaves your device, we will describe it clearly here.
